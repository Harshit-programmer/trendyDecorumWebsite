Namaste & Welcome

Bringing India's Soul & Celebration to Canada

Founded and curated by Monika in Oshawa, Ontario, Trendy Decorum began with a simple desire: to make high-quality, authentic, and vibrant Indian festive decorations accessible to families across Canada and North America without compromise.

Every piece in our catalogue is thoughtfully handpicked directly from artisans across India. Whether it is a hand-painted Pichwai cow door hanging, an intricately embroidered gota toran, or a shimmering marigold garland, our collection is curated with devotion to Indian heritage, auspicious symbolism, and refined aesthetic taste.

We take pride in offering friendly 1-on-1 customer service. Whether you're looking for a single statement hanging for your front door or coordinating return favors for a 300-guest wedding, we are always here to help you style your celebration.
