# Trendy Decorum: Website Questionnaire

Hi! These answers will help us build a website that fits your business.
Questions marked ⭐ are the most important. Skip anything you're unsure about.

---

## 1. About You & the Business
1. ⭐ What's the official business name? Is it registered (sole proprietor, corporation)?
2. ⭐ Which city/province are you based in?
3. How long have you been running Trendy Decorum?
4. What's the story behind it? Why did you start it? (We'll use this for an "About" page.)
5. Would you like your photo or name on the site, or should it stay brand-only?
6. What makes you different from other Indian decor sellers in Canada? (Price, quality, unique designs, custom work, fast delivery?)

## 2. Products
7. ⭐ What do you sell? Tick all that apply and add anything missing:
   - [ ] Diyas / candles / tea-light holders
   - [ ] Torans / door hangings / bandhanwars
   - [ ] Rangoli (stickers, powder, mats)
   - [ ] Lanterns / hanging lights / fairy lights
   - [ ] Pooja thalis / pooja items / idols
   - [ ] Wall hangings / mirrors / home decor
   - [ ] Backdrops / mandap / event decor
   - [ ] Artificial flowers / garlands (marigold, etc.)
   - [ ] Urli bowls / floating candles
   - [ ] Gift hampers / return gifts / favours
   - [ ] Other: ________
8. ⭐ About how many different products do you have right now? (10? 50? 300?)
9. ⭐ What's your rough price range? (e.g. $5 to $200)
10. How often do you get new stock? Do items sell out and never come back?
11. Do you do **custom orders** (custom colours, names, bulk sizes)?
12. Do you **rent** decor (e.g. backdrops for events), or only sell?
13. Do you offer **decorating services** (setting up homes/venues/stores)?

## 3. Customers
14. ⭐ Who buys from you most? (Households, stores/businesses, event planners, temples, weddings?)
15. Do you sell **wholesale/bulk** to stores? Should the site have a separate wholesale enquiry form?
16. Where are most of your customers? (Your city only, your province, all of Canada, the USA?)

## 4. How You Want to Sell
17. ⭐ Which of these do you want?
   - **A) Online shop.** Customers add items to a cart and pay on the website.
   - **B) Catalogue + enquiry.** Customers browse products and then message you (WhatsApp, Instagram, phone or a form) to order.
   - **C) Start with B now, move to A later.**
18. ⭐ How do customers pay you today? (E-transfer, cash, card, PayPal?)
19. ⭐ Delivery options: local pickup? Local delivery (which areas)? Shipping across Canada?
20. How do you charge for delivery/shipping? Is there a minimum for free delivery?
21. What's your return/exchange policy?
22. Are you registered for GST/HST? (This affects tax at checkout.)

## 5. Festivals & Seasons
23. ⭐ Which occasions matter most for sales? (Diwali, Navratri/Dussehra, Karva Chauth, Raksha Bandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi, Holi, Eid, Lohri, Vaisakhi, weddings, baby showers, housewarmings, Christmas?)
24. Would you like **pre-orders** before big festivals?
25. Do you run sales/discount codes? Should the site support them?
26. Do you set up at pop-ups, markets or mela events? Should we list upcoming events?

## 6. Look & Feel (Branding)
27. ⭐ Do you have a logo? Please send the highest-quality file you have.
28. Do you have brand colours? If not, which feel do you prefer?
   - Rich and festive (deep maroon, gold, marigold orange)
   - Modern and minimal (cream, soft gold, white)
   - Bright and colourful
29. Send 2 or 3 websites (any business) whose look you like, and say what you like about them.
30. Do you have a tagline? (e.g. "Bringing India's festive spirit home")
31. Should the site be in English only, or also in French, Hindi, Punjabi or Gujarati?

## 7. Photos & Content
32. ⭐ Do you have good product photos? (Instagram photos are fine to start with.)
33. Can you share a product list (name, price, short description, sizes/colours)? A spreadsheet is ideal.
34. Do you have customer reviews or testimonials we can use? (Screenshots of DMs work too.)
35. Should we show your **Instagram feed** on the website?
36. Any photos of decor set up in homes/stores/events? (These sell really well.)

## 8. Contact Details
37. ⭐ Which contact options should be public? (Phone, WhatsApp, email, Instagram, address?)
38. Do you have a store/showroom customers can visit, or is it by appointment/home-based?
39. What are your business hours?
40. Any other social accounts? (Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest?)

## 9. Technical & Budget
41. ⭐ Do you already own a domain name (e.g. trendydecorum.ca)? If not, which name do you want?
42. ⭐ What monthly budget is OK for running the site?
   - ~$0 to $5/mo (simple catalogue site)
   - ~$40 to $60/mo (full online shop like Shopify, plus card fees)
43. ⭐ Who will update products and prices? How comfortable are you with tech? (1 = not at all, 5 = very)
44. Do you want customers to sign up for a newsletter/email list for festival offers?

## 10. Goals & Timeline
45. ⭐ What's the #1 thing this website should do for you? (More orders, look more professional, cut down on DMs, reach wholesale clients, rank on Google?)
46. ⭐ When do you need it live? (Note: Diwali 2026 is **Nov 8**, so a quick basic version first may be smart.)
47. Anything you **don't** want on the site?
48. Anything else we should know?

*Thank you! 🪔*
